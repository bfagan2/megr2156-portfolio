# A2 – Truss Stress Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this exercise is to put into practice the theoretical understanding of stress and strain in a physical context. The student is to design a theoretical 2D truss system out of A500 grade steel. Throughout the process, the student will perform calculations of individual member stresses as well as internal forces and shear stress on the pin connections. Safety factors will be used in deciding the minimum cross-sectional area for each identical member. This page of the portfolio will house hand drawings and calculations, as well as CAD drawings that serve as documentation of the design process. Each step of the process will be explained in detail for the purpose of replication.
