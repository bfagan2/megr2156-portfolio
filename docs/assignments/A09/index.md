# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective

The objective of this assignment was to design a pulley system using reasonable assumptions made using our best judgement. For this assignment, I decided to create a spreadsheet in Excel rather than doing the calculations by hand. This saved me a lot of time and allowed me to change my assumptions without having to redo my math. The screenshots included in this portfolio are from different iterations of the same Excel sheet, with assumptions for each part included in the "Knowns" and "Unknowns" sections.


### Part 1

Part 1 involved selecting a belt from McMaster-Carr using the dimensions that were solved for. In my Excel calculations, I assumed a pulley separation distance of 4 in, rotational speed of 1000 rpm, and pulley material of aluminum. ### Part 3

Part 3 involved designing a journal bearing to support the pulley shafts in the system. Using the equations and process developed last week in A8, I calculated P and V from the given values in the assignment. The length of the journal bearing had to be assumed to be able to solve the equations. My first assumption of 0.5 inches yielded a PV rating that was far too low to be matched with any material from the table in the Machinery's Handbook. I changed the assumption of length to 0.25 inches which allowed for a PV rating of 4188.
